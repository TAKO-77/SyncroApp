# Product Requirements Document
# 01 Product Overview
- **Product Name:** Syncro
- **Tagline:** Organize your life across all your devices from one unified workspace.
- **Description:** Syncro is a cross-platform application (available natively on PC and mobile) designed to help users schedule events, track time-sensitive tasks, and capture future ideas. It consolidates productivity tools into a single, elegant interface to facilitate a more organized and pleasant life.

# 02 Problem
Users currently fragment their daily planning across multiple disconnected apps—using one tool for calendars, another for daily to-do lists, and yet another for project ideation. This creates friction, especially when switching between mobile devices and desktop computers.

# 03 Goal
Establish a seamless, cross-platform productivity ecosystem that allows users to manage their daily agendas, tasks, and calendar events from a single codebase application.

# 04 Target Users
- Professionals needing to track tight schedules and priority tasks across desktop and mobile.
- Students balancing assignment deadlines, class schedules, and personal projects.
- Individuals seeking a centralized system to organize their daily routines and future goals.

# 05 Core Features (Version 1)
- **Home:** The start screen, with a summary of every tab: today's agenda, the most urgent tasks, this month's balance and the next special days, each linking to its tab.
- **Calendar:** A centralized view to schedule, manage, and visualize upcoming events and appointments.
- **Agenda:** A dedicated daily and weekly breakdown of scheduled activities and time-blocks.
- **To-do Tasks:** A task tracking system supporting effort levels, priority tags, and due dates categorized by workspaces.
- **Profile Tab:** A user management area for settings, workspace configuration, and theme preferences (Light/Dark mode).

# 06 Out of Scope (for now)
- Gym workout planning and exercise tracking.
- Complex third-party calendar integrations (if not supported natively by the initial database schema).
- Advanced team collaboration features.

# 07 Success Metrics
- High percentage of daily active users utilizing both the mobile and PC versions.
- High task completion rate indicating effective organization.
